# **App Name**: FAU-G Domination

## Core Features:

- Battlefront Hero Section: An immersive cinematic experience featuring high-definition auto-playing gameplay footage to introduce players to the world of FAU-G.
- Operation Modules: Visual breakdown of various game modes with detailed descriptions and dynamic card hover effects showing specific objective data.
- Tactical Armory: An interactive library of Characters, Weapons, and Maps using image carousels and attribute badges to display combat statistics.
- Elite Intelligence Bureau: Dedicated dossiers for Brand Ambassadors, Mentors, and Executive Leadership including CEO bios and company histories with image-rich layouts.
- Vanguard Creator Hub: A centralized gallery showcasing official content creators and game achievements with direct links and social proof indicators.
- AI Tactical Loadout Tool: A tool that uses generative AI to recommend specific character and weapon pairings based on map types and chosen gameplay modes.
- Connectivity Gateway: Integration of social links with custom-styled YouTube and Instagram logos and a button leading to an immersive 3D variant of the platform.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Tactical Gold (#E6AC1A), evoking achievement and high-stakes military prestige.
- Background Color: Obsidian Slate (#1F1D19), a deeply desaturated hue providing a premium dark aesthetic.
- Accent Color: Flare Orange (#D43B12), providing a high-contrast analogous pop for CTAs and critical alert elements.
- Headlines feature 'Space Grotesk' for its techy, futuristic combat feel. Body text uses 'Inter' for machined, high-legibility objective information.
- Vector-based military glyphs with subtle glowing outlines to reinforce the high-tech modern warfare aesthetic.
- A multi-sectioned 'one-page' layout using split-screens for Asset previews and expansive hero headers for video content.
- Sophisticated 3D parallax scrolling and scanline-inspired UI transitions to create a cinematic HUD atmosphere.
